Traffic advisory: Diljit Dosanjh concert, Exhibition Ground, Sector 34, Chandigarh, Saturday 14 December 2024.

The road to the ground will be closed to vehicles from 11:00 AM. Venue entry opens at 3:00 PM and the show starts at 7:00 PM. About 20,000 people are expected.

No parking will be allowed near the ground. Visitors must park at the Sector 17 multilevel parking, Dussehra Ground Sector 43, the ground opposite Laxmi Narayan Mandir Sector 44, Dussehra Ground Sector 45 and Mandi Ground Sector 29. Two-way shuttle buses will run from these parking areas. Cabs must drop passengers at the parking areas.

Traffic restrictions apply from 4:00 PM. Commuters should avoid the Sector 33/34 dividing road, Piccadilly Chowk and New Labour Chowk. Traffic will be diverted at Gaushala Chowk, the Sector 44/45 light point and the Bhawan Vidyalaya School T-point.
