# December 2024: the Sector 34 concerts

## Why this file exists

This is the real case the project is built around. It collects what was reported, with sources, so the demo and the pitch rest on facts. Everything here comes from the news reports linked at the bottom. Where the reports are silent, this file says so.

## Summary

In December 2024, Chandigarh hosted two large concerts a week apart at the Exhibition Ground in Sector 34: Karan Aujla on 7 December and Diljit Dosanjh on 14 December. Both choked the roads around the ground, which sits in a busy area of shops, offices, coaching centres and hospitals. After complaints from residents, traders and doctors, the administration decided not to allow big gatherings at Sector 34 in future, and moved AP Dhillon's 21 December concert to the Rally Ground in Sector 25. That show also caused inconvenience. In August 2025, the UT Tourism Department proposed a new concert venue at Sarangpur with its own parking.

## Timeline

| September 2024 | Permission was granted for the Diljit Dosanjh (14 December) and AP Dhillon (21 December) concerts at the Sector 34 Exhibition Ground. |
| Sat 7 Dec 2024 | **Karan Aujla concert, Sector 34.** Over 20,000 fans attended. There were ten parking zones in Sector 34, plus the Sector 17 multilevel parking with a shuttle. The police advisory restricted or diverted roads from 4 PM, kept two stretches near the ground open only to ticket holders' vehicles from 5 PM, and gave emergency vehicles priority. On the day, a diversion ran from 2:30 PM until the show started, Piccadilly Chowk and New Labour Chowk saw chaos, coaching students and office workers were held up, and the same happened when the crowd left. |
| 12 Dec 2024 | The Chandigarh Beopar Mandal (a traders' body) and a delegation that included former mayor Arun Sood, doctors from the Indian Medical Association, a councillor and the Sector 34 Furniture Market Association met Deputy Commissioner Nishant Kumar Yadav. They asked for the Diljit and Dhillon shows to be moved to the Sector 25 Rally Ground. They cited business losses after the Aujla show, traffic jams, and an area full of shops, offices, hospitals and schools that the ground can't serve at this scale. |
| 13 Dec 2024 | The administration decided in principle not to allow huge gatherings at the Sector 34 ground in future, but let the Diljit and Dhillon shows go ahead. Reports cited residents saying they were effectively stuck at home during the Aujla show, four private hospitals and one medical college and hospital nearby, and traders' losses. The UT police chief chaired a meeting on the expected traffic disruption. |
| 13 Dec 2024 | The Punjab and Haryana High Court allowed the Diljit concert on a public interest petition. The petition had cited the 7 December chaos, which hindered traffic, emergency services and daily life. The court capped noise at 75 dB(A) at the ground's boundary, and reports say the show had to end by 10 PM. |
| 13 Dec 2024 | **Police traffic plan for Diljit:** no parking near the ground. Five parking areas with two-way shuttle buses: the Sector 17 multilevel parking and adjoining lots, the Dussehra Ground in Sector 43, the open ground opposite Laxmi Narayan Mandir in Sector 44, the Dussehra Ground in Sector 45, and the Mandi Ground in Sector 29. Cabs had to drop passengers at these lots. Restrictions applied from 4 PM. Commuters were told to avoid the Sector 33/34 dividing road, Piccadilly Chowk and New Labour Chowk. Diversions were set at Gaushala Chowk (towards Faidan or Kajheri Chowk), the Sector 44/45 light point (towards South End or Gurudwara Chowk) and the Bhawan Vidyalaya School T-point (towards the Sector 33/45 Chowk). |
| Sat 14 Dec 2024 | **Diljit Dosanjh concert, Sector 34** (Dil-Luminati tour). The road to the ground was closed to vehicles from 11 AM, and the slip road towards Sham Fashion Mall by noon. Entry opened at 3 PM. Cars were parked in Sector 21 near a petrol pump and on the road opposite the ground. A commuter was stuck in a jam at the Sector 32 junction. Fans complained the shuttle service was poor and had to walk from the lots, and things were no better after the show. A few young fans were injured and struggled to get help, because private vehicles weren't allowed near the ground. Two nearby filling stations and the market lost business. The crowd size was not reported. |
| 18 Dec 2024 | The administration moved AP Dhillon's show to the Rally Ground, Sector 25, after residents and traders complained about traffic chaos, noise and lost business during the two Sector 34 shows. |
| 19 Dec 2024 | **Police plan for Dhillon:** no parking at or near the venue. Three parking areas with shuttles: the Sector 17 multilevel parking, the Dussehra Ground in Sector 43, and the Grain Market Ground in Sector 39. The Sector 25/38 and Sector 14/25 dividing roads were restricted from 4 PM up to the Dhanas turn. Congestion was expected at the Sector 14/15/24/25 Chowk, Bhaskar Chowk, Dadu Majra light point and Yatri Niwas Chowk. Commuters were told to use Madhya Marg and Dakshin Marg. |
| Sat 21 Dec 2024 | **AP Dhillon concert, Sector 25.** Noise stayed around 80 dB for most of the show, above the 75 dB limit. A report in August 2025 says this show also caused inconvenience. |
| 29 Dec 2024 | Noise readings at the Diljit show ranged from 76.1 to 93.1 dB against the 75 dB cap, and the administration was likely to recommend a penalty of up to Rs 15 lakh. A committee led by the Deputy Commissioner was drafting a standard operating procedure (SOP) for large gatherings, covering noise monitoring, traffic management and keeping inconvenience to residents low. |
| 24 Mar 2025 | **Honey Singh concert, Sector 25 Rally Ground.** Police assigned parking by direction of arrival: vehicles from the Faidan side, Gaushala Chowk and Mohali went to the Dussehra Ground in Sector 43, and vehicles from the TPT light point, Nayagaon, Kansal and Zirakpur went to the Sector 17 multilevel parking. QR codes were issued for parking, with shuttles and no drop-offs near the venue. |
| 13 Aug 2025 | The UT Tourism Department proposed a convention-cum-exhibition centre on over 25 acres at Sarangpur, mainly for big concerts. One design holds about 35,000 people and another about 25,000, both with parking for 6,250 cars. The design went for approval, to be followed by a project report and a tender. The report describes no traffic plan for the new venue. |

## What went wrong for traffic, in plain words

These points are drawn from the reports above. None of the reports include measurements.

1. **The venue sits in a busy area.** Event traffic and normal traffic to shops, offices, coaching centres and hospitals fought for the same roads.
2. **The disruption lasted most of the day.** For the Diljit show, the road to the ground closed at 11 AM, hours before the show.
3. **Parking didn't match how people behaved.** People parked in Sector 21 and on the road opposite the ground instead of in the official lots.
4. **Shuttles weren't enough,** so the walk from the lots was long.
5. **Emergency access suffered.** Injured fans struggled to get help.
6. **Leaving was as bad as arriving.**
7. **The fix was trial and error.** The city moved the venue after two difficult nights, and the next venue also caused inconvenience.

## What the reports don't tell us

- Diljit's crowd size.
- Each parking lot's capacity, and how many shuttles ran.
- How long the jams were and how long they lasted.
- How the traffic plans were drawn up, and whether any plan was tested before the day.
- Whether the SOP for large gatherings was finished. We found no report of it.

The simulation fills these gaps with the assumptions marked in `01-simulation-inputs.md`.

## How the demo uses this case

1. **Plan A, the real plan:** rebuild the police plan for 14 December 2024 in the simulator, with its closures, five parking areas, shuttles and diversions.
2. **Sanity check:** the simulation should show trouble at the places the reports name: Piccadilly Chowk, New Labour Chowk and the Sector 32 junction. This is a basic check, not proof.
3. **The agent's plan:** let the agent search for a better plan for the same concert at Sector 34.
4. **The venue question:** run the same crowd at Sector 25 with the plan police used for Dhillon, and compare.
5. **Looking ahead (if time):** rehearse the proposed Sarangpur venue, with about 35,000 people and 6,250 parking spaces, before it is built.
